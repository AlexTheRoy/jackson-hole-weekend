    Pick the river day

    DAY 3

    The real whitewater near Coeur d’Alene in midsummer is the Clark Fork through Alberton Gorge, two hours east in Montana. The Spokane River is the easy, close-to-town alternative.

    The run

      Water through the season
      May and JuneSnowmelt season. ROW runs the Spokane, St. Joe and Lochsa. The Spokane has its Class III+ water in late May and June. The Clark Fork trips have not started.
      July 1 to about July 10Clark Fork season opens with higher flows. ROW’s minimum age is 12 until the water drops.
      Mid-July through AugustPrime Clark Fork season. ROW lists the water at 62 to 68°F and runs daily from July 19 to August 29 in 2027. The Spokane is a warm, gentle float.
      SeptemberROW’s 2027 Clark Fork season ends August 29. Ask about late trips.

      Alberton Gorge is listed as runnable from about 1,200 to 20,000 cfs. Your outfitter decides on the day.

            When to go

            Late July through August lines everything up: daily Clark Fork trips, snow-free trails, a warm lake and the driest weeks of the year. The tradeoffs are crowds and a chance of wildfire smoke.
