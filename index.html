    Pick the raft

    DAY 3

    Every outfitter runs the same 7.9-mile stretch from West Table to Sheep Gulch, with Class II–III rapids like Big Kahuna and Lunch Counter. What changes is the boat.

    The run, rapid by rapid

    Mileage and class ratings are American Whitewater’s. Numbers match the dots on the River map, and the rapids are labeled on the 3D model when you open the River canyon view.

      Water through the season
      Late May to mid-JunePeak snowmelt. The biggest, pushiest and coldest water of the year.
      Late June and JulyFlows ease toward medium. A trip report from the end of June 2025 called about 6,000 cfs a medium flow.
      AugustFlows keep easing after the snowmelt peak. More splash than push.
      SeptemberLate-season water. A September 2022 report logged about 4,300 cfs. Cooler air and a quieter river.

      The run is listed as runnable from about 1,800 to 20,000 cfs. Your outfitter decides on the day.
